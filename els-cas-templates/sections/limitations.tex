\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}
The evidence is limited to MVTec, VisA, and MPDD. The frozen DINOv2 PCA residual is an inherited ranking substrate, while official AnomalyDINO and reported WinCLIP results use different protocols and serve only as external references. The controlled shift study covers four synthetic corruptions, not the full range of temporal drift, sensor replacement, process changes, correlated failures, or deployment prevalence. Moreover, scoring latency is measured on cached DINOv2 features and excludes backbone inference, preprocessing, and I/O. Generalization to prospective factory operation therefore requires evaluation under real shifts and end-to-end deployment constraints.

Target-only conformal validity requires representative normal supports and the relevant exchangeability conditions. LOIO calibration and full-support test residuals are asymmetric, and their p-values occupy a finite grid; our clustered discrete-reference analyses diagnose departures from the exchangeable pattern but do not restore validity when exchangeability fails. ECE is additionally sensitive to prevalence and binning, so it remains descriptive rather than a false-alarm guarantee. The FGSM results define a separate boundary: robustness to the evaluated corruptions does not imply adversarial robustness.

The certification claims are narrower still. Historical CRESS results reuse source evidence and are empirical diagnostics, whereas the independent strict evaluation returns $\tau=0$ for every category-certified target cell because only three or four certification categories are available. The distribution-free all-zero lower bound explains why additional images or seeds cannot repair this category shortage; assumption-dependent parametric or hierarchical alternatives would require their own validity arguments. Fixed-archive image certificates target an archive mixture, not a new-category risk. Finally, matched source selection assumes reliable condition metadata, and source-to-target control still requires source-risk dominance or category exchangeability. Thus the current evidence supports an auditable source-assisted procedure and its feasibility boundary, not a deployable certified alarm for an arbitrary target category.
