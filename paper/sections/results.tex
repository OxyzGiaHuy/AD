\section{Results}
\subsection{Clean Accuracy and Storage}
\input{tables/tab_clean_efficiency}
Table~\ref{tab:clean-efficiency} summarizes clean image-level performance and storage. On MVTec, CalibSubspaceHead is competitive but does not consistently exceed memory-bank baselines. Its main advantage is compactness: PCA64 stores about 0.472 MB, compared with roughly 2--6 MB for the memory-bank rows in our table. On VisA, the detector is stronger relative to PatchCore/AnomalyDINO-style baselines in our benchmark. The PCA128 ablation improves VisA AUROC by 0.011--0.017 over PCA64 while keeping storage below 0.6 MB, giving a useful accuracy-storage tradeoff.

\subsection{Full VisA Conformal Reliability}
\input{tables/tab_visa_full_conformal}
Table~\ref{tab:visa-full-conformal} gives the main reliability result. Across 56,000 full VisA corruption rows, LOIO conformal obtains ECE 0.0766 while preserving the same PCA/subspace ranking. The strongest regime is $k=4$, where ECE drops to 0.0391. At $k=8$, ECE increases to 0.1140 but remains better than Platt-style baselines in every corruption cell. This behavior is interpretable: increasing $k$ improves anomaly separation and AP, but normal samples also receive higher conformal anomaly confidence, making calibration less conservative.

\input{tables/tab_visa_ece_by_corruption}
Table~\ref{tab:visa-ece-corruption} isolates corruption effects. LOIO conformal improves over Vector Platt and Shift-Aware Platt for blur, brightness/contrast shift, Gaussian noise, and JPEG at both $k=4$ and $k=8$. Weighted conformal is a useful ablation but not a safe default: it is competitive for selected $k=8$ structured corruptions, yet it is much worse at $k=4$ and under Gaussian noise.

\subsection{Routing, Pixel Localization, and Fragility Diagnostics}
\input{tables/tab_protocol_routing}
Table~\ref{tab:protocol-routing} evaluates protocol-locked representative routing. Fixed LOIO conformal reduces ECE relative to Vector Platt without using validation labels to select an expert. This supports the central CRR claim: reliability routing can be useful even when the route is fixed and conservative. SAGE-style gating remains a promising design inspiration, but the current main evidence favors simple LOIO routing over learned or weighted expert selection.

\input{tables/tab_pixel_and_diagnostics}
Pixel-level results show that the low-storage subspace detector is also competitive for localization. Robustness diagnostics are less favorable: FGSM experiments show severe fragility and objective sensitivity. These results are important negative evidence and motivate reliability diagnostics, but they do not support an adversarial robustness claim.

\subsection{Novelty Guardrails}
\input{tables/tab_prior_work_positioning}
Table~\ref{tab:prior-positioning} summarizes the claim boundary. The official SubspaceAD representative run is especially important because it reaches high image and pixel AUROC in our protocol.
