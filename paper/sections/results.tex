\section{Results}
\subsection{Clean Accuracy and Storage}
\input{tables/tab_clean_efficiency}
Table~\ref{tab:clean-efficiency} summarizes the clean image-level and storage evidence. On MVTec, CalibSubspaceHead is competitive but does not consistently exceed memory-bank baselines. Its value is the storage tradeoff: the PCA64 variant stores about 0.472 MB, while memory-bank baselines in our table range from roughly 2 MB to 6 MB. On VisA, the same detector is stronger relative to PatchCore/AnomalyDINO-style baselines in our benchmark. The PCA128 ablation improves VisA AUROC by 0.011--0.017 over PCA64 while keeping storage below 0.6 MB, supporting an accuracy-storage Pareto claim.

\subsection{Full VisA Conformal Reliability}
\input{tables/tab_visa_full_conformal}
Table~\ref{tab:visa-full-conformal} shows the full VisA conformal reliability result. LOIO conformal achieves an overall ECE of 0.0766 over 56,000 image rows. The result is strongest at $k=4$ (ECE 0.0391), while $k=8$ remains improved over Platt-style baselines but has higher ECE (0.1140). This $k=8$ caveat is caused by normal samples receiving higher conformal anomaly probabilities; anomaly confidence also increases, so ranking and AP improve slightly while calibration becomes less conservative.

\input{tables/tab_visa_ece_by_corruption}
Table~\ref{tab:visa-ece-corruption} compares ECE under each corruption. LOIO conformal improves over Vector Platt and Shift-Aware Platt in every tested cell. Weighted conformal is mixed: it is competitive at $k=8$ for blur, brightness/contrast, and JPEG, but is poor at $k=4$ and under Gaussian noise. We therefore keep weighted conformal as an ablation rather than the main reliability layer.

\subsection{Routing, Pixel, and Robustness Diagnostics}
\input{tables/tab_protocol_routing}
Table~\ref{tab:protocol-routing} summarizes protocol-locked representative routing. Fixed LOIO conformal improves ECE without validation-label expert selection, supporting the claim that the conformal view itself carries useful reliability information. This is the safest SAGE-inspired story: routing is used to organize reliability views, but the main reported route is fixed and does not rely on test-label expert choice.

\input{tables/tab_pixel_and_diagnostics}
Pixel-level results show that the low-storage subspace detector is also competitive for localization. However, FGSM experiments show severe fragility and objective sensitivity. We therefore report adversarial experiments as failure diagnostics, not as robustness improvements.

\subsection{Novelty Guardrails}
\input{tables/tab_prior_work_positioning}
Table~\ref{tab:prior-positioning} lists what we avoid claiming. The official SubspaceAD representative run is particularly important: it reaches high image and pixel AUROC in our local protocol, so our paper should not frame DINOv2 PCA residual ranking as the contribution. The contribution is reliability routing and calibration diagnostics around that low-storage ranking model.
