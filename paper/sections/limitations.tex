\section{Limitations}
The current version is a strong internal draft but not yet a final submission. First, the method is not an MVTec AUROC SOTA claim. Official SubspaceAD remains a very strong accuracy baseline, and our MVTec value is mainly storage, calibration, and diagnostics. Second, the method is not adversarially robust: FGSM tests show severe score collapse and objective sensitivity. Third, LOIO conformal reliability depends on representative normal support images. When support images are too few or not exchangeable with test normals, p-values can become overconfident. Fourth, the full conformal result is strongest on VisA $k=4$; at $k=8$, ECE remains better than Platt baselines but normal-sample overconfidence increases. Finally, full MVTec conformal routing and broader no-label deployment studies should be completed before making universal cross-dataset reliability claims.
