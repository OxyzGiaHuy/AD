\section{Conclusion}
We presented a decoupled reliability-routing framework for low-storage few-shot industrial anomaly detection. The detector preserves frozen DINOv2 PCA/subspace residuals for anomaly ranking and adds separate calibration and conformal reliability views for probability-style decision support. The main empirical finding is that LOIO conformal reliability substantially reduces ECE on a full VisA corruption benchmark while preserving the raw subspace ranking. The paper's strongest current claim is therefore not pure AUROC superiority, but reliable, low-storage decision support under few-shot and shifted inspection conditions. Future work should expand full MVTec conformal routing, refine no-label gates, and connect conformal reliability to operator-facing false-alarm control.
