\section{Conclusion}
We presented a decoupled reliability view for low-storage few-shot industrial anomaly detection. By preserving DINOv2 PCA/subspace residuals for ranking and adding LOIO conformal p-values for reliability, the pipeline improves calibration under full VisA corruptions while keeping the method storage-light and interpretable. The strongest current evidence supports conformal reliability routing as the main contribution, with weighted and gated variants serving as ablations and diagnostics.
